Illustrative scenarios

Patterns I keep seeing.

Based on common situations in business development. These are not client results.

Scenario

From tender to partnership

Situation
A buyer compares tenders on price alone.
Move
Add a clear compliance file, references and a named contact.
Lesson
Documents are part of the offer.
Scenario

Prequalification as a growth asset

Situation
Every new buyer asks for the same documents.
Move
Build one organised library once and update it every quarter.
Lesson
Preparation compounds.
Scenario

Turning follow-up into a system

Situation
Leads go quiet after the first meeting.
Move
Use a simple pipeline where every lead has a dated next step.
Lesson
Speed of follow-up signals respect.
