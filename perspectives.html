Questions & reflections

Questions worth asking.

No answers here, only better questions. Pick one and sit with it for five minutes.

01

What is one conversation you could start today?

02

Who deserves a follow-up from you this week?

03

What assumption are you about to test?

04

What would make your next proposal easier to say yes to?

05

Which task could a simple system do for you?

06

What did you learn this week that others would pay to know?

07

What is the one thing you will finish before you start something new?

08

What if the assumptions shaping your decisions are the very things limiting your possibilities?

09

What would you try if you knew the obvious answer was wrong?

10

Which pattern are you seeing that others have not named yet?

11

What connects two ideas you have always kept separate?

12

What is the smallest version of this idea you could build this week?
